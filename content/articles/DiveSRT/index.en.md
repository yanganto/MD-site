# DiveSRT – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

> **Summary:** DiveSRT does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All processing happens entirely on your device.

---

## 1. Overview

DiveSRT is a utility application that converts Garmin dive computer data files into SRT subtitle files. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for users of the App.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App does **not**:

- Collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
- Access, read, or transmit your dive data files beyond your device
- Connect to any remote server or cloud service
- Use analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising libraries
- Request unnecessary device permissions

## 3. How the App Works

All file processing — reading your Garmin dive data and generating SRT subtitle files — is performed entirely **locally on your device**. No data ever leaves your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services

DiveSRT does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## 5. Children's Privacy

DiveSRT does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will update the Effective Date above. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
